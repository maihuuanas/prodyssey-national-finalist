
# Prodyssey '25 — National Finalist | IIM Indore

## Overview
Selected as a **National Finalist** in Prodyssey, a national-level Product Management case competition held during **Atharv Ranbhoomi '25** at **IIM Indore**, competing among 1,000+ teams from across the country. The competition was organized in association with Yousta, AceMBA, and NTPC.

## About the Competition
Prodyssey is a case-based Product Management competition that challenges participants to solve real-world business and product problems using structured frameworks, market analysis, and data-driven decision-making — evaluated by industry professionals and academic experts.
